HOW IT WORKS

From your hangar and back, in four steps.

  1. 01
    Request & ship
    Send us engine type, P/N and S/N. We confirm and you ship the nozzles.
  2. 02
    Incoming inspection
    Visual inspection and documentation check on arrival.
  3. 03
    Test & repair
    Flow test and spray pattern check on the FNTS, repair or overhaul as needed.
  4. 04
    Release & return
    Released with EASA Form 1 and shipped back to you.

ABOUT US

A specialist Part 145 shop for P&WC fuel nozzles.

XL Turbine is an EASA Part 145 approved maintenance organisation (HR.145.051) based in Zagreb, Croatia. Our core work is inspection, testing, repair, overhaul and exchange of fuel nozzles for Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6A, PT6T, PT6C and PW200 engines – backed by our own fuel nozzle test stand and exchange stock – complemented by independent pre-buy inspections.
