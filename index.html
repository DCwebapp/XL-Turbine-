HOW IT WORKS

From your hangar and back, in four steps.

    Request & ship
    Send us engine type, P/N and S/N. We confirm and you ship the nozzles.
  2. 02
    Incoming inspection
    Visual inspection and documentation check on arrival.
  3. 03
    Test & repair
    Flow test and spray pattern check on the FNTS, repair or overhaul as needed.
  4. 04
    Release & return
    Released with EASA Form 1 and shipped back to you.

ABOUT US

A specialist Part 145 shop for P&WC fuel nozzles.

XL Turbine is an EASA Part 145 approved maintenance organisation (HR.145.051) based in Zagreb, Croatia. Our core work is the inspection, testing, repair, overhaul, and exchange of fuel nozzles for Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6A, PT6T, PT6C, and PW200 engines – backed by our own advanced fuel nozzle test stand and a robust exchange stock – complemented by independent pre-buy inspections.

Founded by a team of deeply committed aerospace professionals, XL Turbine brings together more than 50 years of collective experience in the aviation industry. Our background spans commercial airlines, business aviation, and component MRO. Over the decades, our experts have successfully managed complex aircraft deliveries and phase-ins, overseen fleet asset management, directed the construction of modern hangar facilities, and established fully compliant EASA CAMO and Part-145 organisations from the ground up.

With deep-rooted expertise in global logistics, strategic procurement, and contract negotiations, we understand the critical importance of minimizing aircraft downtime. Today, we channel this massive, multi-disciplinary experience into a highly focused, premium component shop—delivering world-class reliability, rapid turnaround times, and uncompromised safety to operators worldwide.
